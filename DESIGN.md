## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Value
The design system projects authority, technological rigor, operational excellence, and uncompromising reliability. Serving enterprise asset management, corporate security, specialized engineering maintenance, and industrial facilities operations, the visual language merges the technical precision of modern engineering with high-touch executive security services.

### Design Aesthetic
The aesthetic follows **Corporate / Modern** with high-contrast, technical underpinnings:
- **Crisp Structural Discipline:** Deep nocturnal slate-navy backgrounds contrast against vivid safety orange accents, drawing directly from tactical equipment and industrial safety standards.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Layouts favor generous hierarchy, razor-sharp alignment, and well-organized operational dashboards over decorative clutter.
- **Balanced Toughness:** Micro-textures, subtle borders, and structural framing evoke physical infrastructure and mission-critical reliability without sacrificing digital sophistication.

## Colors

The chromatic strategy relies on high-visibility operational contrast:

- **Primary (`#E25C1D` / `#FF6B00`):** Vibrant safety orange, serving as the high-impact focal point for calls-to-action, key service badges, active statuses, and structural iconography.
- **Secondary (`#081221`):** Deep nocturnal navy representing executive security, authority, and corporate grounding. Used for structural navigation, dark hero cards, and high-contrast typography.
- **Tertiary (`#2563EB`):** Engineering blue, utilized selectively for secondary data visualizations, technical metrics, and verified certifications.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Architectural slate providing legible muted labels, structural borders, and neutral secondary surfaces.

### Surface System
- Default presentation runs on a clean, luminous base (`#F8FAFC` and `#FFFFFF`) punctuated by dark navy hero containers (`#081221`) to reflect high-stakes operational control centers.
- Borders use crisp slate tones (`#E2E8F0` on light surfaces, `#1E293B` on dark containers) to delineate technical service modules cleanly.

## Typography

The typographic pairing strikes a balance between solid architectural presence and high-density interface readability:

- **Primary Display & Headings (Montserrat):** Bold geometric proportions convey strength, reliability, and precision. `label-caps` is utilized with extended tracking (`0.12em`) to label divisions, operational protocols, and category tags (e.g., `FACILITIES`, `PORTARIA & CONTROLE`).
- **Body & Data Content (Inter):** High x-height, neutral grotesk clarity ensures technical service descriptions, maintenance logs, operational reports, and form fields maintain rapid scanning speed across all devices.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Philosophy
The system implements a rigid 12-column responsive fluid grid designed around an 8px base rhythm:
- **Desktop (>=1200px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `4rem` outer section margin, max content boundary capped at 1280px for standard views and 1440px for operational dashboards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `2rem` outer margin.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1rem` outer margin.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section wrappers leverage `space-2xl` for executive pacing, while dense operational lists, metric blocks, and facility modules use `space-sm` and `space-md` internally to prevent disconnected fragmentation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a hybrid of **Tonal Layers** and **Subtle Structural Shadows**:

1. **Flat Base / Level 0:** `#FFFFFF` canvas or `#081221` dark mode backgrounds with crisp `#E2E8F0` or `#1E293B` 1px borders.
2. **Elevated Cards / Level 1:** Background `#FFFFFF` (or `#0F1E36` in dark panels) with a calibrated soft ambient shadow: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(8, 18, 33, 0.06)`, bounded by a subtle border (`1px solid rgba(8, 18, 33, 0.08)`).
3. **Interactive & Hover / Level 2:** `0px 12px 28px -4px rgba(8, 18, 33, 0.12)`, with an optional 1.5px orange top accent border on high-priority service cards.
4. **Overlays & Modals / Level 3:** `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(8, 18, 33, 0.25)` complemented by a frosted dark backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background: rgba(8, 18, 33, 0.75)`).

## Shapes

The design uses **Soft (Level 1)** geometric radii:
- Standard elements (buttons, form inputs, chips) feature `0.25rem` (4px) or `0.375rem` (6px) corners, echoing industrial hardware and architectural panels rather than consumer pill-shapes.
- Cards, service containers, and dialogs apply `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Status indicator tags and micro-badges use tight `0.25rem` corners to preserve structured line integrity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#E25C1D` background, `#FFFFFF` bold typography, `0.375rem` corner radius, `space-sm` vertical by `space-lg` horizontal padding. Hover shifts to `#C2410C` with a slight upward translate (`-1px`).
- **Secondary Corporate:** Solid `#081221` background, `#FFFFFF` text, subtle outline. Hover lightens to `#0F1E36`.
- **Outline / Service Action:** Transparent background, 1.5px border `#081221` (or `#E25C1D`), crisp high-contrast text.

### Service & Capability Cards
- Built with high visual distinction: 1px border with a soft neutral fill or deep navy surface.
- Top section houses a prominent geometric icon (e.g., access control turnstile, maintenance gear, cleaning shield) containerized in a `#E25C1D` tinted badge (`rgba(226, 92, 29, 0.1)`) with crisp `#E25C1D` stroke iconography.
- Typography includes an all-caps division pre-header in `label-caps`, followed by a bold `headline-sm` title and concise `body-sm` scope details.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Compact height (24px - 28px) with `0.25rem` radius.
- Categorical chips use subtle gray fills (`#F1F5F9`) with slate typography (`#1E293B`).
- Active operational statuses (e.g., "Operação Ativa", "Monitoramento 24h") use a glowing pulse indicator alongside high-contrast badge fills.

### Input Fields & Selects
- 44px standard height for touch and desktop efficiency.
- 1px border `#CBD5E1` transitioning to a 2px `#E25C1D` focus ring with zero ambient spread.
- Floating or distinct uppercase `label-caps` for strict legibility in enterprise procurement and budget request workflows.

### Operational KPI & Metric Tiles
- Dark navy surface (`#081221`) with sharp internal padding (`space-lg`).
- Numerical value displayed in `headline-xl` (Montserrat) with primary orange highlight for key digits, anchored by secondary metrics in slate gray.
